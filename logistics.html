Responsible carrier selection

Compliance & verification

Requirements depend on the route, cargo, and applicable jurisdiction. Before presenting a carrier for a shipment, relevant items may include:

  • Business registration and applicable operating authority
  • Vehicle registration, inspections, and maintenance records
  • Commercial motor and cargo insurance appropriate to the work
  • Driver qualifications and any route- or cargo-specific permits
  • Safety procedures and shipment handling requirements

We are building our partner network and verification process. No carrier credentials or regulatory approvals are claimed on this page. Customers and carriers should confirm applicable requirements and contract terms before transport.
