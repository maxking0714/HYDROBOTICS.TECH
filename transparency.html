HYDROBOTICS TECH · MODERATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Transparency Statement

Last updated 6 October 2026. This statement describes the moderation design of the HYDROBOTICS browser demo; it is not a platform-wide regulatory transparency report.

Our rules

The Terms of Service prohibit hate speech and discrimination, harassment and cyberstalking, threats and incitement, child exploitation, terrorist propaganda, illegal content, unlawful defamation, doxxing, and other violations of rights. We aim to protect people from harm while respecting lawful expression. A platform-rule decision is not itself a legal finding.

How moderation works in this demo

Users can report feed posts, community listings, conversations, accounts, or other concerns. Reports are saved in this browser and appear in the local admin queue. An administrator can review the report, remove a feed post, or block an account. This demo does not automatically detect harmful content, prioritize urgent reports operationally, contact authorities, or provide a staffed response team.

When a post or account action is recorded, the affected account can see the case ID under Settings → Moderation decisions. Removed post content is retained in that browser's moderation record so an administrator can restore it if an appeal is reversed. This is a prototype behavior, not a production retention standard.

Appealing a decision

  1. Sign in with the account affected by the decision.
  2. Open Settings → Moderation decisions and choose “Appeal this decision”.
  3. Explain why the decision should be changed, including relevant context.
  4. An administrator can uphold the decision or reverse it. Reversing a post removal restores its archived post in this demo; reversing an account block removes that block.

The appeal and its outcome are recorded locally. This prototype does not provide an independent reviewer, external notice, or response deadline.

Production commitments

A real service would need documented reviewer procedures, timely user notices, reasoned decisions, an effective independent appeal path, audited aggregate reporting, secure evidence handling, and jurisdiction-specific legal review. This local demo does not provide those operational safeguards.
