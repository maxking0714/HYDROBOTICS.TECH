Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register?

Open the Login page, choose Create Account, and verify your email address. Supabase handles account credentials; setup instructions are in the Supabase auth setup guide.

How do I reset my password?

Choose “Forgot your password?” on the Login page, enter your account email, and follow the reset link we send you.

Can I post updates?

Yes. After logging in, use the Social Feed page to create posts and add them to the feed.

Is data saved?

Supabase Auth handles account credentials when configured. Other social and community preview content may still be static or stored in this browser; do not use it to store sensitive information.

Are other site features private?

No. Account authentication does not protect the static or browser-stored social features. Do not enter sensitive information until those features use a secured backend.

What pages are available?

Use Home, About, FAQ, Feed, Login, Dashboard, Account, Settings, Videos, Chat, Companies, Inventor, and Admin pages in this demo.
