HYDROBOTICS TECH · COMMUNITY RULES

Terms of Service

These community rules apply to posts, comments, profile content, uploads, proposals, and messages shared through HYDROBOTICS. This page is a prototype policy, not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific terms.

Use the platform lawfully and respectfully

You may not create, upload, request, share, or promote content that is unlawful or that violates another person's rights. This includes:

  • Hate speech, discriminatory abuse, or dehumanizing attacks targeting protected or vulnerable groups.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse, grooming, or any sexual content involving a child. Never upload or redistribute such material.
  • Terrorist recruitment or propaganda, instructions or encouragement for violent attacks, or incitement to imminent violence.
  • Fraud, impersonation, malware, unauthorized access, or other illegal activity.
  • Knowingly false factual claims presented to damage a person's or organization's reputation, or other unlawful defamation.
  • Private or identifying information shared without authorization, and content that infringes intellectual property or confidentiality rights.

These rules are platform standards and do not replace applicable law or limit lawful expression. Content may be restricted under these rules even when a legal determination has not been made.

Report concerns

Use the Report action on feed posts, community listings, or chat conversations, or open the safety report form. Select the closest category and provide enough context for review. Do not include unrelated personal data or circulate illegal material as evidence.

Reports enter the local admin review queue in this demo. Administrators can review reports, remove content, or block accounts using the admin console. This prototype is not continuously monitored and cannot guarantee response times or emergency intervention. Contact local emergency services if there is immediate danger.

Moderation

Depending on the concern and available evidence, content or access may be removed, limited, or blocked. Users can contest an active decision from Settings → Moderation decisions. An administrator can uphold or reverse it; reversing a content removal restores its archived post in this demo. See the Transparency Statement for details. This static demo does not perform external escalation, age verification, evidence preservation, independent appeals review, or legally timed takedowns.

Prototype limits

HYDROBOTICS in this workspace is a browser-only demonstration. Reports, moderation, and account restrictions are stored locally and are not sent to a staffed safety team. Do not rely on this demo to report an urgent threat or submit sensitive evidence.
